PERSONAL SAFETY

G101 -- Humans, stay off the FIELD during the MATCH.
Description Other than actions explicitly allowed under Human rules (G428-G434), a DRIVE TEAM member may only enter the FIELD: (A) during pre-MATCH set-up to place their ROBOT and pre-loaded SCORING ELEMENTS, and (B) after a MATCH is over, to stop and collect their ROBOT in a reasonable amount of time when instructed by the Head REFEREE or designee.
Penalty Verbal Warning
Notes A team may not delay the FIELD reset process through an excessively lengthy removal process. It is not a violation if DRIVE TEAM members contribute to FIELD reset by placing SCORING ELEMENTS they inadvertently moved while setting up. Egregious violations (e.g., entering the FIELD during a MATCH) are covered by G211.
G102 -- Be careful when interacting with ARENA elements.
Description A team member may not: (A) climb on, (B) hang from, (C) manipulate an ARENA element so it does not return to its original shape without human intervention, or (D) damage an ARENA element.
Penalty Verbal Warning Yellow Card if subsequent violations occur during the event
Notes DRIVE TEAM members may brace the FIELD perimeter at any point during the MATCH. Moving the FIELD perimeter out of position is considered a violation of G102.C.

CONDUCT

G201 -- Be a good person.
Description All teams must be civil toward everyone and respectful of team and event equipment while at a FIRST Tech Challenge event. Review the FIRST Code of Conduct and Core Values.
Penalty Verbal Warning Yellow Card if subsequent violations occur during the event
Notes Examples of behavior likely to result in ARENA ejection include: assault, threats, harassment, bullying, insulting, swearing at another person, and yelling in anger/frustration.
G202 -- DRIVE TEAM interactions.
Description DRIVE TEAM members cannot distract or interfere with the opposing ALLIANCE. This includes taunting or other disruptive behavior.
Penalty Verbal Warning Yellow Card if subsequent violations occur during the event
G203 -- Asking other teams to throw a MATCH – not cool.
Description A team may not encourage an ALLIANCE of which it is not a member to play beneath its ability. (Not intended to prevent an ALLIANCE from planning/executing its own strategy in a MATCH where all teams are members of that ALLIANCE.)
Penalty Verbal Warning Red Card if subsequent violations occur during the event
G204 -- Letting someone coerce you into throwing a MATCH.
Description A team, as a result of encouragement by a team not on their ALLIANCE, may not play beneath its ability. (Not intended to prevent an ALLIANCE from planning/executing its own strategy where all ALLIANCE members are participants.)
Penalty Verbal Warning Red Card if subsequent violations occur during the event
G205 -- Throwing your own MATCH is bad.
Description A team may not intentionally lose a MATCH or sacrifice RANKING POINTS to lower their own ranking and/or manipulate the rankings of other teams.
Penalty Verbal Warning Red Card if subsequent violations occur during the event
G206 -- Don't violate rules for RPs.
Description A team or ALLIANCE may not collude with another team to each purposefully violate a rule in an attempt to influence RANKING POINTS.
Penalty Yellow Card ALLIANCE ineligible for PATTERN and GOAL RPs
G207 -- Do not abuse ARENA access.
Description A team member (other than DRIVE TEAM members for that MATCH) granted access to restricted areas in/around the ARENA (e.g., media badges) may not assist, coach, or use signaling devices during the MATCH. Exceptions for inconsequential infractions and safety.
Penalty Verbal Warning Yellow Card if subsequent violations occur during the event
Notes Team members in open-access spectator seating are not in a restricted area and are not prevented from assisting or signaling.
G208 -- Show up to your MATCHES.
Description If a ROBOT has passed initial, complete inspection, at least 1 DRIVE TEAM member must report to the ARENA and participate in each assigned Qualification MATCH.
Penalty Disqualified from the current MATCH
G209 -- Keep your ROBOT together.
Description A ROBOT may not intentionally detach or leave a part on the FIELD.
Penalty Red Card
G210 -- Do not expect to gain by doing others harm.
Description Actions clearly aimed at forcing the opponent ALLIANCE to violate a rule are not allowed. The targeted ALLIANCE will not receive a penalty for the forced violation.
Penalty Minor Foul Major Foul if REPEATED No penalty assigned to the targeted ALLIANCE
Notes Does not apply to strategies consistent with standard gameplay (e.g., pushing an opponent into an ARTIFACT while accessing your GATE). Requires an intentional act with limited/no opportunity for the targeted team to avoid the penalty (e.g., pushing an opponent from far away into their own LOADING ZONE; placing an ARTIFACT into an opponent ROBOT to cause a G408 violation).
G211 -- Egregious or exceptional violations.
Description Egregious behavior beyond what is listed in the rules, or repeated violations of any rule/procedure during the event, is prohibited. The Head REFEREE may assign a YELLOW or RED CARD for egregious ROBOT actions or team member behavior at any time.
Penalty Yellow Card Red Card (if severe)
Notes Behaviors that automatically result in a card because they put the community at risk include, but are not limited to: (A) inappropriate behavior per the G201 orange box, (B) reaching into the FIELD and grabbing a ROBOT during a MATCH, (C) a single PIN in excess of 15 seconds, and (D) descoring SCORING ELEMENTS strategically or REPEATEDLY. Continued violations are escalated to FIRST Headquarters.
G212 -- All teams can play.
Description A team may not encourage another team to exclude their ROBOT or be DISQUALIFIED from a Qualification MATCH for any reason.
Penalty Yellow Card Red Card if the ROBOT does not participate in the MATCH

PRE-MATCH

G301 -- Be prompt.
Description A DRIVE TEAM member may not cause a significant delay to the start of their MATCH. A significant delay requires BOTH: (A) the expected MATCH start time has passed, and (B) the DRIVE TEAM has ARENA access and is neither MATCH ready nor making a good-faith effort, as perceived by the Head REFEREE, to quickly become MATCH ready.
Penalty Verbal Warning (Qual) / Verbal Warning to ALLIANCE (Playoff) Major Foul for the upcoming MATCH if subsequent within the tournament phase Robot Disabled if not MATCH ready within 2 minutes of the warning/foul and no good-faith effort
Notes Qualification expected start time = scheduled time or ~3 min after the previous MATCH on that FIELD, whichever is later. Playoff expected start time = scheduled time or 8 min after either ALLIANCE'S previous MATCH, whichever is later. 'MATCH ready' = ROBOT on the FIELD in STARTING CONFIGURATION and powered on, with DRIVE TEAM in starting positions. Good-faith efforts include walking to the FIELD, quick tape/cable-tie fixes, waiting for a DRIVER STATION to boot, working with the FTA, and a MOMENTARY wiggle test.
G302 -- Limit what you bring to the FIELD.
Description Items brought to the FIELD beyond the ROBOT/OPERATOR CONSOLE must fit in the ALLIANCE AREA, be worn/held by DRIVE TEAM, or be an accommodation item. Regardless, items may not: (A) introduce a safety hazard, (B) extend more than 6 ft. 6 in. above the TILES, (C) communicate with anything outside the ARENA, (D) block visibility for FIELD STAFF/audience, or (E) jam/interfere with anything in the ARENA.
Penalty Match will not start until the situation is remedied Yellow Card if discovered or used inappropriately during a MATCH
Notes Alignment devices used to aid pre-MATCH setup are allowed as long as they don't delay MATCH start (G301). Items with wireless disabled comply with (C).
G303 -- ROBOTS on the FIELD must come ready to play.
Description A ROBOT must: (A) not pose a hazard to humans, FIELD elements, or other ROBOTS; (B) have passed inspection; (C) if modified after inspection, comply with I305; (D) be the only team-provided item left on the FIELD; (E) show the correct ALLIANCE color on its SIGN; and (F) be motionless after OpMode initialization.
Penalty Match will not start until requirements are met (if quick remedy) Disabled if not a quick remedy; Red Card if a noncompliant ROBOT (B or C) participates
Notes If DISABLED prior to MATCH start, the DRIVE TEAM may not remove the ROBOT without Head REFEREE/FTA permission.
G304 -- ROBOTS must be set up correctly on the FIELD.
Description A ROBOT must: (A) be over a LAUNCH LINE, (B) touch its own GOAL or the FIELD perimeter, (C) be fully contained on its own ALLIANCE'S side of the FIELD, (D) not be attached to/entangled with/suspended from any FIELD element, (E) be in STARTING CONFIGURATION, and (F) be in contact with no more than the allowed pre-load possession limit.
Penalty Match will not start until setup is corrected (if quick remedy) Disabled if not a quick remedy
Notes G304.C requires the ROBOT be fully contained within the FIELD perimeter and not overhang the wall.
G305 -- Teams must select an OpMode.
Description An OpMode must be selected on the DRIVER STATION app and initialized (INIT). If an AUTO OpMode, the 30-second AUTO timer must be enabled.
Penalty Match will not start until the situation is remedied Disabled if the ROBOT cannot initialize an OpMode or the issue can't be quickly remedied
Notes Required even if a team does not plan to run an AUTO OpMode — FIELD STAFF use INIT as a readiness signal.

AUTO

G401 -- Let the ROBOT do its thing.
Description Once FIELD STAFF begins randomization and until AUTO ends, DRIVE TEAM members may not directly or indirectly interact with their ROBOT or OPERATOR CONSOLE except to: (A) press start within a MOMENTARY reaction of MATCH start, (B) press stop at the team's discretion or REFEREE instruction, or (C) for personal/console safety.
Penalty Major Foul ALLIANCE ineligible for PATTERN points in AUTO if an ARTIFACT enters the open top of the GOAL after the interaction and before AUTO ends
Notes FIELD STAFF will not re-randomize the OBELISK due to violations of this rule prior to MATCH start. Strategic violations of G401.A are considered egregious behavior under G211.
G402 -- No AUTO opponent interference.
Description During AUTO, FIELD columns A-C are blue's side and D-F are red's side. A ROBOT may not: (A) contact an opposing ROBOT that is completely within the opponent's side, directly or transitively through an ARTIFACT, or (B) disrupt a pre-staged ARTIFACT on the opponent's side, directly, transitively through ARTIFACT contact, or by LAUNCHING an ARTIFACT directly into it.
Penalty Major Foul per instance of ROBOT contact (G402.A) Major Foul per ARTIFACT disrupted (G402.B)
Notes LAUNCHED ARTIFACTS that end up on the other side after deflecting off another object are not penalized. Example: a LAUNCH that misses the GOAL, deflects off the structure, and disrupts pre-staged ARTIFACTS on the far side is not a violation.

TELEOP

G403 -- ROBOTS are motionless between AUTO and TELEOP.
Description Any powered movement of the ROBOT or its MECHANISMS is not allowed during the AUTO-to-TELEOP transition.
Penalty Major Foul
Notes Movement from inertia, gravity, or de-energizing actuators is not a violation. Teams may stop AUTO / INIT or start TELEOP during the transition, but INIT must not cause motion (wait for TELEOP if it would). LAUNCHING an ARTIFACT during the transition is a violation. Strategic violations (e.g., LAUNCHING multiple elements, operating the GATE, moving a substantial distance) are egregious behavior under G211.
G404 -- ROBOTS are motionless at the end of TELEOP.
Description ROBOTS must have no powered movement after TELEOP ends until the Head REFEREE/designee signals retrieval is allowed.
Penalty Minor Foul Major Foul per ARTIFACT that enters the open top of a GOAL after TELEOP Major Foul if the ROBOT contacts a GATE after TELEOP
Notes DRIVE TEAMS should press the stop (■) button or otherwise clearly stop operating the ROBOT. Movement from inertia/gravity/de-energizing is not powered movement.

SCORING ELEMENTS

G405 -- ROBOTS use SCORING ELEMENTS as directed.
Description A ROBOT may not deliberately use a SCORING ELEMENT to ease or amplify a FIELD challenge in a way not intended by the game design.
Penalty Major Foul per SCORING ELEMENT
Notes Examples: positioning SCORING ELEMENTS to impede opponent access to FIELD elements; placing them in inaccessible locations (e.g., under the RAMP/GOAL); using one to hold the GATE open.
G406 -- Keep SCORING ELEMENTS in bounds.
Description A ROBOT may not intentionally eject a SCORING ELEMENT from the FIELD, directly or by bouncing off a FIELD element or another ROBOT.
Penalty Major Foul per SCORING ELEMENT
Notes SCORING ELEMENTS that leave the FIELD during normal scoring attempts are not intentional ejections.
G407 -- Do not damage SCORING ELEMENTS.
Description Neither a ROBOT nor a DRIVE TEAM member may damage a SCORING ELEMENT.
Penalty Verbal Warning Major Foul if REPEATED Disabled if ROBOT-caused damage is likely to recur
Notes Corrective action (removing sharp edges, removing the offending MECHANISM, re-inspection) may be required before competing again. Reasonable wear/scratching is expected; routinely gouging, tearing, or marking elements violates this rule.
G408 -- No more than 3 at a time.
Description A ROBOT may not simultaneously CONTROL more than 3 ARTIFACTS.
Penalty Minor Foul per SCORING ELEMENT over the limit Yellow Card if excessive
Notes Not CONTROL: bulldozing, deflecting, or incidental contact while acquiring from the LOADING ZONE; LAUNCHED elements no longer touching the ROBOT. "Excessive" = 5+ ARTIFACTS at once, or 3+ separate greater-than-MOMENTARY violations of 4+ ARTIFACTS in a MATCH. REPEATED excessive violations do not result in additional YELLOW CARDS unless egregious enough to trigger G211.

ROBOT

G409 -- ROBOTS must be under control.
Description A ROBOT must not pose an undue hazard to a human or ARENA element: (A) the ROBOT or anything it CONTROLS disrupts anything outside the FIELD or contacts a human outside the FIELD, or (B) ROBOT operation is dangerous.
Penalty Disabled Verbal Warning Yellow Card if REPEATED or subsequent during the event
Notes Examples: flailing outside the FIELD, knocking over a DRIVER STATION stand, damaging the FIELD timer display, contacting FIELD STAFF/DRIVE TEAM outside the FIELD. Contact with ARENA elements outside the FIELD (stand, floor, outer wall) is not a violation. Disrupting the OBELISK is not a violation of this rule.
G410 -- ROBOTS must stop when instructed.
Description If instructed to DISABLE by a REFEREE, a DRIVE TEAM member must press the stop (■) button on the DRIVER STATION app.
Penalty Major Foul for greater-than-MOMENTARY delay Red Card if CONTINUOUS
G411 -- ROBOTS must be identifiable.
Description A ROBOT'S team number and ALLIANCE color must not become indeterminate, as judged by the Head REFEREE.
Penalty Verbal Warning Minor Foul if subsequent violations occur during the event
G412 -- Don't damage the FIELD.
Description A ROBOT may not damage FIELD elements.
Penalty Verbal Warning Disabled if further damage is likely Yellow Card for any subsequent damage during the event
Notes SCORING ELEMENT damage is covered by G407 (G407 and G412 don't stack); normal gameplay damage is exempt. FIELD damage includes contaminating the FIELD with liquid/fine solid, damaging a TILE, or bending/breaking a GATE. It does not include a GATE that normally "sticks" open or a GOAL lifting off the TILES from normal interaction.
G413 -- Watch your ARENA interaction.
Description A ROBOT may not grab, grasp, attach to, become entangled with, or suspend from an ARENA element (except SCORING ELEMENTS per G407).
Penalty Major Foul Yellow Card if REPEATED or greater-than-MOMENTARY Disabled if further damage is likely
Notes ROBOTS operating the GATE should push the lever down to open; no closing force (e.g., pulling) should be applied.
G414 -- ROBOTS have horizontal expansion limits.
Description ROBOTS must comply with horizontal expansion limits (R105.A). Over-expansion due to damage, not used for strategic benefit, is exempt.
Penalty Minor Foul Major Foul if used for strategic benefit or affects scoring
G415 -- ROBOTS have vertical expansion limits (with exceptions).
Description ROBOTS must comply with vertical expansion limits (R105). Expansion above 18 in. up to 38 in. is allowed only during the final 20 seconds of the MATCH and only when not in any LAUNCH ZONE.
Penalty Minor Foul Major Foul if used for strategic benefit or affects scoring
G416 -- LAUNCHING in the LAUNCH ZONE only.
Description ROBOTS may only LAUNCH SCORING ELEMENTS when inside a LAUNCH ZONE or overlapping a LAUNCH LINE.
Penalty Minor Foul per LAUNCHED SCORING ELEMENT Major Foul per LAUNCHED SCORING ELEMENT that enters the open top of the GOAL
Notes "LAUNCHING" = shot into the air, propelled to a desired location/direction, or thrown forcefully. "Bulldozing" is not LAUNCHING. Not intended to penalize normal manipulator operation (e.g., reversing an intake, herding an element a short distance).
G417 -- ROBOTS only operate GATES as directed.
Description ROBOTS may not: (A) contact, directly or transitively through a SCORING ELEMENT, an opposing ALLIANCE'S GATE, or (B) apply, directly or transitively, any closing force to either GATE.
Penalty Major Foul Opposing ALLIANCE awarded the PATTERN RP if G417.A
Notes Closing force = any force in the closing direction, even on an already-closed GATE. Bumping a stuck-open GATE handle to try to close it is not a closing force. (Unlike an earlier version of this rule, the penalty is a single MAJOR FOUL, not assessed per ARTIFACT released by the GATE.)
G418 -- ROBOTS may not meddle with ARTIFACTS on RAMPS.
Description ROBOTS may not contact, directly or transitively through a CONTROLLED SCORING ELEMENT, ARTIFACTS on any RAMP (including their own). Additionally, ROBOTS may not: (A) remove an ARTIFACT from their own RAMP except by operating the GATE, or (B) remove an ARTIFACT from the opponent's RAMP by any means.
Penalty Major Foul per ARTIFACT ALLIANCE ineligible for PATTERN RP if G418.A Opposing ALLIANCE awarded PATTERN RP if G418.B
Notes Inconsequential, inadvertent contact while operating a GATE is exempt. Example: opening the opponent's GATE causing 5 ARTIFACTS to leave their RAMP results in 6 MAJOR FOULS total (1 under G417.A + 5 under G418.B) plus the opponent is awarded the PATTERN RP.
G419 -- ROBOTS LAUNCH into their own GOAL.
Description ROBOTS may not: (A) intentionally place or LAUNCH ARTIFACTS directly onto their own RAMP, or (B) place or LAUNCH ARTIFACTS into the opponent's GOAL or onto the opponent's RAMP.
Penalty Major Foul per ARTIFACT Opposing ALLIANCE awarded the PATTERN RP if G419.B
Notes Intended scoring is LAUNCHING into the open top of your own GOAL. No violation for scoring in an opponent's DEPOT.

OPPONENT INTERACTION

Note – G420 and G421 are mutually exclusive.
A single ROBOT-to-ROBOT interaction that violates more than one of G420/G421 results in only the most punitive penalty being assessed.
G420 -- This is not combat robotics.
Description A ROBOT may not deliberately functionally impair an opponent ROBOT. Damage/impairment from contact with a tipped-over or DISABLED opponent, not perceived as deliberate, is not a violation.
Penalty Major Foul Yellow Card Major Foul + Red Card if the opponent ROBOT is unable to drive
Notes Examples of functional impairment: disconnecting internal wires, disconnecting the opponent's battery, or powering off an opponent via a well-protected switch (these also result in a RED CARD since the ROBOT can no longer drive). Powering off an opponent via an exposed, unprotected switch during normal gameplay is considered incidental, not deliberate. "Unable to drive" means the DRIVER can no longer reach a desired location in a reasonable time.
G421 -- Do not tip or entangle.
Description A ROBOT may not deliberately, as perceived by a REFEREE, attach to, tip, or entangle an opponent ROBOT.
Penalty Major Foul Yellow Card Major Foul + Red Card if CONTINUOUS or the opponent ROBOT is unable to drive
Notes Examples: using a wedge to tip an opponent; frame-to-frame contact with a ROBOT trying to right itself; contacting a tipping ROBOT when contact could have been avoided. Tipping as an unintended consequence of normal interaction (including single frame-to-frame hits) is not a violation.
G422 -- There is a 3-count on PINS.
Description A ROBOT may not PIN an opponent for more than 3 seconds. PINNING = preventing an opponent's movement by direct or transitive contact while the opponent is attempting to move. A PIN count ends when: (A) ROBOTS separate by 2 ft. for more than 3 sec., (B) either ROBOT moves 2 ft. from where the PIN started for more than 3 sec., or (C) the PINNING ROBOT itself gets PINNED.
Penalty Minor Foul Additional Minor Foul for every 3 seconds not corrected
Notes For (A)/(B), the count pauses (rather than resets) once the separation/movement condition is met, and resumes if ROBOTS come back within range.
G423 -- Do not use strategies intended to shut down major parts of gameplay.
Description A ROBOT or ROBOTS may not, in a REFEREE'S judgment, isolate or close off any major element of MATCH play for a greater-than-MOMENTARY duration.
Penalty Minor Foul Additional Minor Foul for every 3 seconds not corrected
Notes Examples: shutting down access to all SCORING ELEMENTS, quarantining an opponent to a small area, quarantining SCORING ELEMENTS out of reach, or completely blocking access to the opponent's GATE.
G424 -- GATE ZONE is OFF LIMITS.
Description A ROBOT may not contact, directly or transitively through a SCORING ELEMENT, an opponent ROBOT if either ROBOT is in the opponent's GATE ZONE, regardless of who initiates. Exception: (A) a ROBOT in its own GATE ZONE that is also in the opponent's SECRET TUNNEL ZONE is not protected under G424 (G425 applies instead).
Penalty Minor Foul
G425 -- Keep out of opponent's SECRET TUNNEL.
Description A ROBOT in the opponent's SECRET TUNNEL ZONE may not contact, directly or transitively through a SCORING ELEMENT, an opponent ROBOT, regardless of who initiates.
Penalty Minor Foul
G426 -- LOADING ZONE protection.
Description A ROBOT may not contact, directly or transitively through a SCORING ELEMENT, an opponent ROBOT while either ROBOT is in the opponent's LOADING ZONE, regardless of who initiates.
Penalty Minor Foul
G427 -- BASE ZONE protection.
Description During the last 20 seconds of the MATCH, a ROBOT may not contact, directly or transitively through a SCORING ELEMENT, an opponent ROBOT while either ROBOT is in the opponent's BASE ZONE, regardless of who initiates.
Penalty Major Foul Opponent ROBOT (and any ROBOT it fully supports) awarded fully-returned-to-BASE points

HUMAN PLAYER

G428 -- No wandering.
Description DRIVE TEAM members must remain in their designated ALLIANCE AREA: (A) may be anywhere within it during a MATCH, and (B) must be staged inside it prior to MATCH start.
Penalty Verbal Warning Minor Foul if subsequent violations occur during the event
Notes Intent is to prevent leaving the AREA for competitive advantage (e.g., better viewing angle, reaching into the FIELD); simply breaking the plane during normal play is not a foul. Members may retrieve SCORING ELEMENTS that left the FIELD if they can do so without violating G428/G430/G434; reintroduction must follow G432. Exceptions for safety and MOMENTARY/inadvertent/inconsequential actions.
G429 -- DRIVE COACHES and other teams: hands off the controls.
Description A ROBOT shall be operated only by its DRIVERS; DRIVE COACHES may not handle gamepads. DRIVE COACHES may only: (A) hold the DRIVER STATION device, (B) troubleshoot it, (C) select OpModes, (D) press INIT, (E) press start (▶), or (F) press stop (■).
Penalty Major Foul Yellow Card if greater-than-MOMENTARY
G430 -- DRIVE COACHES, SCORING ELEMENTS are off limits.
Description DRIVE COACHES may not contact SCORING ELEMENTS, unless for safety purposes.
Penalty Minor Foul
G431 -- DRIVE TEAMS, watch your reach.
Description Once a MATCH starts, a DRIVE TEAM member inside the FIELD may not: (A) directly contact a ROBOT, (B) contact a SCORING ELEMENT in contact with a ROBOT, (C) disrupt SCORING ELEMENT scoring, or (D) contact a FIELD element.
Penalty Major Foul + Yellow Card if G431.A Red Card + Opposing ALLIANCE awarded PATTERN RP if G431.C
Notes Exceptions for safety and MOMENTARY/inadvertent/inconsequential actions. For A/B, the penalty applies regardless of who initiates contact. Impacting ARTIFACT scoring (C) includes: (A) contacting an opponent-LAUNCHED ARTIFACT within the FIELD, (B) contacting an ARTIFACT in the opponent's GOAL, or (C) disrupting scoring of an ARTIFACT on the opponent's RAMP or via the opponent's GATE. The prior example covering a DRIVE TEAM member LAUNCHING an ARTIFACT into a GOAL has been removed from this list.
G432 -- Humans, only meddle with ARTIFACTS in the LOADING ZONE.
Description DRIVE TEAM members may only introduce, remove, or move ARTIFACTS within the LOADING ZONE, and only: (A) during TELEOP, (B) without a tool, (C) without causing an ARTIFACT to enter the LOADING ZONE from elsewhere on the FIELD, and (D) without causing an ARTIFACT to leave the LOADING ZONE for the rest of the FIELD — unless it is CONTROLLED by a ROBOT that (i) began CONTROL while in the LOADING ZONE and (ii) still CONTROLS it upon leaving.
Penalty Minor Foul per ARTIFACT Major Foul per ARTIFACT that enters the open top of the GOAL
Notes Members may load SCORING ELEMENTS into a ROBOT partially or fully in the LOADING ZONE. Unintentional deflections (e.g., a member protecting themselves from a LAUNCHED ARTIFACT) are exempt.
G433 -- Humans may only enter SCORING ELEMENTS.
Description DRIVE TEAM members may only enter ARTIFACTS onto the FIELD.
Penalty Minor Foul per non-ARTIFACT item entered onto the FIELD
G434 -- The ALLIANCE AREA has a storage limit.
Description During TELEOP, an ALLIANCE may not store more than 6 ARTIFACTS out of play. A good-faith effort to immediately re-enter additional ARTIFACTS is exempt.
Penalty Minor Foul per ARTIFACT over the limit Additional Minor Foul per ARTIFACT over the limit every 3 seconds until corrected
Notes Not enforced during AUTO/transition; at TELEOP start, DRIVE TEAMS must make a good-faith effort to get compliant. Not a violation if FIELD STAFF return out-of-FIELD ARTIFACTS causing a temporary excess — but the team must then make a good-faith effort to re-enter them. Intentionally losing access to ARTIFACTS (e.g., removing them from the FIELD/ALLIANCE AREA) is egregious behavior under G211.
