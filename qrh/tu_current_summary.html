What changed: your old QRH (Team Update 12) → the current manual (Team Update 32)

This page summarizes the substantive differences found between the pages you had cached (last synced to Team Update 12, Dec 4-5) and Section 11 Game Rules (G), pages 95-117, of the official DECODE Competition Manual, Team Update 32 — verified directly against the PDF. Purely cosmetic rewording and added examples that don't change a rule's substance are omitted or only briefly noted.

RuleStatusWhat changed
G101 / G102MISSING FROM YOUR SITE The manual has an "11.1 Personal Safety" section ahead of Conduct that your site never had its own page for. G101 covers when humans may enter the FIELD (this is the rule your old G202 summary text actually described — a mismatch dating back to at least Team Update 03). G102 covers not climbing on / hanging from / permanently deforming / damaging ARENA elements, including a note that moving the FIELD perimeter out of position is a G102.C violation (added by Team Update 10v2, Nov 14, 2025). These rules existed in the manual all along; they just weren't broken out into their own page on your site.
G202CHANGED MEANING Your old copy's G202 description ("a human may only enter the FIELD...") is now G101. The current G202 is a different rule: DRIVE TEAM members may not distract or interfere with the opposing ALLIANCE (taunting, disruptive behavior). Penalty structure (Verbal → Yellow) is unchanged, but the rule text itself is not.
G211MISSING FROM YOUR COPY Lists behaviors that will automatically escalate to a card: (A) G201-orange-box-level inappropriate behavior, (B) reaching into the FIELD and grabbing a ROBOT during a MATCH, (C) a single PIN in excess of 15 seconds, and (D) strategic/REPEATED descoring — plus a note that continued violations get escalated to FIRST HQ. I could not find a specific Team Update that added this text, so it may simply have been present all along and left out of your original page rather than changed mid-season.
G303MINOR FIX Team Update 18 (Jan 8, 2026) corrected an internal rule cross-reference in G303.E (ROBOT SIGN ALLIANCE color) from R101 to R402. No practical effect on the rule itself.
G405No substantive change found Your cached copy already had the same three examples (blocking opponent access, inaccessible placement, propping the GATE) that Team Update 15 (Dec 18, 2025) is recorded as adding — so despite the TU record, there's no practical difference between your old page and the current one here.
G409NEW NOTE (TU18) Team Update 18 (Jan 8, 2026) added: "Disrupting the OBELISK is not a violation of this rule." This line is not in your cached copy and is genuinely new since your TU12 baseline.
G301CLARIFIED "Significant delay" is now explicitly a two-part test (expected start time has passed AND no good-faith readiness effort). Expected start time is now spelled out: ~3 minutes after the previous MATCH on that FIELD for Quals, 8 minutes after either ALLIANCE'S previous MATCH for Playoffs (or the scheduled time, whichever is later). "MATCH ready" and "good faith effort" are both now explicitly defined with examples (walking to the FIELD, quick tape/cable-tie fixes, waiting on a DRIVER STATION boot, working with the FTA, a MOMENTARY wiggle test).
G408CONFIRMED (per TU12) Your TU12 summary already flagged this: REPEATED excessive-CONTROL violations do not stack additional YELLOW CARDS — this is now the settled text (unless a violation is separately egregious enough to trigger G211).
G412CLARIFIED FIELD damage now has explicit examples: contaminating the FIELD with liquid/fine solid, damaging a TILE, or bending/breaking a GATE. Explicitly excludes a GATE that normally "sticks" open or a GOAL lifting off the TILES from ordinary interaction.
G417CONFIRMED Your robot.html already carried a manual note that this rule no longer assesses a foul "per artifact" released by the GATE. Directly verified against the TU32 PDF: the violation is a single MAJOR FOUL (plus PATTERN RP loss if G417.A), not one per ARTIFACT. Your loaded.html copy still had the older "NOTE: CONSIDER G418" annotation instead — that inconsistency has been resolved in the pages below.
G419No rule change found Your TU12 summary flagged uncertainty here. Comparing text directly: current wording is functionally identical to what you had (MAJOR FOUL per ARTIFACT, PATTERN RP lost to the opponent under G419.B). No substantive change located beyond very minor rewording.
G420 / G421Expanded examples only Both rules picked up explicit example lists (disconnecting wires/battery, powering off via a protected switch, using a wedge MECHANISM, contact with a self-righting ROBOT) and a shared definition of "unable to drive." No penalty structure change.
G431CONFIRMED Your old QRH's TU12 summary guessed at this and it's now directly verified against the TU32 PDF: the example "a DRIVE TEAM member LAUNCHING an ARTIFACT into a GOAL" has been removed from the list of actions that count as impacting ARTIFACT scoring under G431.C. Only three examples remain (contacting an opponent-LAUNCHED ARTIFACT in the FIELD, contacting an ARTIFACT in the opponent's GOAL, and disrupting RAMP/GATE scoring). The rule text itself (A-D, including "contact a FIELD element") is unchanged.
G432NEW EXCEPTION (TU15) Team Update 15 (Dec 18, 2025) added an exception for unintentionally deflected ARTIFACTS (e.g., a DRIVE TEAM member protecting themselves from a LAUNCHED ARTIFACT), plus a reminder note about practicing coordination to avoid G431.A contact. Neither line is in your cached copy.
Everything else (G201, G203-G210, G212, G302, G304-G305, G401-G407, G410-G411, G413-G416, G418, G422-G430, G433-G434)No substantive change found Wording has been tightened in places and additional illustrative examples/figures have been added throughout the current manual, but the rule descriptions, structure, and penalties match what you already had.
